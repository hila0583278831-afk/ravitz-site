What we collect

When you send a meeting request we receive your full name, phone number, email address, the option you choose under "I am" and your message. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you share there. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site securely.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a meeting and, if you become a client, provide our property-management services. You are not legally required to give us any information, but without contact details we cannot reply. We will not send you marketing messages unless you separately agree to receive them.

Who receives it

RAVITZ staff who handle your request, and service providers acting on our behalf: FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the form to our email inbox, and GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, your message is also processed by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 24 months. Client information is kept as long as required by our agreement and by Israeli law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission, restricted access and two-factor authentication on our accounts.

Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law you may request access to your information and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. If you are in the EU or UK you also have rights to object, restrict processing and data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, email office@zravitz.com.

Testimonials

Testimonials and the video on this site are published with the consent of the people who appear in them.

ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

RAVITZ believes everyone should be able to use our website easily, independently and with dignity. This website was adjusted under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Act, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and aims to meet Israeli Standard SI 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0, level AA).

Adjustments on this site

  • Keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators; the menu and video window close with the Esc key.
  • Headings, landmarks and labelled controls for screen readers.
  • Text descriptions for content images; decorative elements are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Animations respect the system "reduce motion" setting.
  • Labelled form fields with required-field markers.
  • The video testimonial includes on-screen English captions.

Known limitations

Some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible, including some scroll-driven animations. If anything is hard to use, contact us and we will provide the information or service another way.
